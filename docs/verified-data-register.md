## Candidate venues

PickleYam Kabacan and multipurpose/basketball courts sometimes used for pickleball are not seeded or publicly listed. Before addition, an administrator must record an official page, owner confirmation, Google Maps evidence, or field verification for every required publication field.

## Publication checklist

- Official/owner-confirmed name
- Complete Kabacan address
- Accurate coordinates and map link
- Indoor/outdoor classification and venue type
- Operating hours
- Rental rate
- Bookable schedule
- Public contact channel
- Actual rights-confirmed court photos
- Available amenities

Unknown information must remain blank. A placeholder must be explicitly labelled and must never imply that a stock image is the venue.
